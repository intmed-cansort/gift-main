13  Numerator and deonominators

Reviewers requested numbers and percentages (numerators and denominators) of eligible and tested.

We had described already in the paper that there were 4946 total eligible relatives across the four arms of the study of whom 948 were invited and 267 were tested.

We chose a cluster randomized design with adjustment for baseline covariates based on a clinical conceptual framework focused on patients and their families and attention to precision of measuring causal effects. The causal effects for this design are estimated by predicted probabilities of testing and invitation conditional on covariate values that isolate the comparison of interest. Given the complexity of the design, it is not possible to present simple numerators and denominators the way it might be for a multi-arm, non-cluster randomized clinical trial with no baseline covariates.

However, we can present the unadjusted proportions which are quite similar to the full model proportions.

13.1 Testing outcome

13.1.1 Cost arm

code 
run sub/load_data.do
qui table costarm,stat(freq) stat(sum test) stat(mean test)
collect label dim costarm "Cost arm", modify
collect label levels costarm 0 "Low cost" 1 "Free"
collect label levels result frequency "Freq." ///
    frequency      "Elgible relatives(N)" ///
    mean   "Tested(proportion)" ///
    total         "Tested(N)" ///
    , modify
collect style cell result[mean], nformat(%5.2f)
collect preview
           |  Elgible relatives(N)   Tested(N)   Tested(proportion)
-----------+-------------------------------------------------------
Cost arm   |                                                       
  Low cost |                 2,491          90                 0.04
  Free     |                 2,455         182                 0.07
  Total    |                 4,946         272                 0.05
-------------------------------------------------------------------
